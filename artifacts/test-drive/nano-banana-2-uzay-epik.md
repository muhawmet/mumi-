## 1 — ONE PIECE (Toei Bold-Cel) · UZAY · "Gökada gemisinin güvertesinde, dev nebula karşısında"

```
In the exact animation style of One Piece (Toei Animation TV anime) — bold flat cel-shaded
anime, NOT a graphic novel, NOT realistic 3D rendering.

A brave young Anatolian sky-sailor, broad-shouldered in a flowing coat, standing on the wooden
deck of a flying galleon-airship, one arm thrown wide, mouth open in an epic shout as a
COLOSSAL swirling nebula fills the sky before him — a cosmic ocean of stars.

RENDER: pure 2D anime cel — uniform thick pure-black outline, zero taper. Flat 2-value cel fill
only (one mid tone + one hard shadow step). Absolutely NO gradient, NO airbrush, NO cross-
hatching, NO realistic skin shading. Saturated poster-primary colors. The nebula is painted as
bold hand-brush cel masses — cream-gold, amber-orange and marine-blue star clouds, NOT a
realistic photographic space, flat graphic anime cosmos filling the top two-thirds of frame.

CAMERA: dramatic low frog-eye angle, 28mm, the sailor towering against the enormous nebula,
horizon (deck edge) low at bottom 25%, epic scale.

TEXT: none.

Keep it looking like a real epic One Piece anime screenshot — flat, graphic, cel-shaded, high
saturation, grand-adventure energy. NO English text.
```

---

## 2 — RICK & MORTY (Adult-Swim Sci-Fi) · UZAY · "Astronot, dev uzay-canavarı karşısında deadpan"

```
In the exact cartoon style of Rick and Morty (Adult Swim) — thin hand-drawn BOILING wobble-
line 2D cartoon. The outline must be shaky and wobbling, hand-inked, NEVER a clean vector,
NEVER smooth SVG, NEVER crisp digital line.

A small tired astronaut in a worn beige spacesuit, noodle limbs, rounded oversized helmet-head,
tiny dot pupils, one bead of sweat, standing deadpan and completely unimpressed on a cracked
alien moon surface — while a COLOSSAL grotesque tentacled space-creature looms in the sky
behind, wet organic body, absurd cosmic horror, staged FLAT.

RENDER: thin 1-2px hand-inked outline that BOILS and wobbles frame-to-frame, never clean, never
vector. Economical noodle-limb anatomy. Flat 1-2 value cel color, murky-olive and beige sci-fi
palette, ONE radioactive toxic-green glow (creature's eye or a portal) as the only light pop.
Dense deadpan alien-biology background gags. NO cinematic depth, NO 3D render, NO smooth vector,
NO painterly grade, NO clean flat SVG look — keep it messy hand-drawn TV animation.

CAMERA: flat two-shot, ~35mm, the tiny deadpan astronaut small in frame against the enormous
looming creature, casual unglamorous vantage.

TEXT: none.

Keep it looking like a real Rick and Morty episode screenshot — messy boiling line, deadpan,
flat institutional color. NO English text.
```
