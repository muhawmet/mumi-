SORUNLARI BİRLİKTE ÇÖZÜYORUZ — CODEX YÖNETMEN PİLOTU
Pilot kapsamı: 01–06 · HIGH-END VIVID FEATURE-ANIMATION
=========================================================================

AMAÇ
Bu pilot mevcut Claude paketinin üstüne yazmaz. Aynı VO ve aynı karakter
referanslarını korur; fakat “her şeyi eşit anlatan oyuncak şehir” görünüşünü
terk eder. Hedef gerçek bir uzun metraj animasyon filminin açılış sekansıdır:
zengin ve yaşayan dünya, yüksek prodüksiyonlu ışık, güçlü sinema kadrajı,
duygusal oyunculuk ve vivid fakat kontrollü renk. Her karede tek bir dramatik
fikir ve baskın geometri vardır. Renk sahneden doğar; bütün karelere aynı
amber/indigo reçetesi uygulanmaz.

NEGATİF YASASI
NEGATIVE alanı hazır bir render yasakları listesi değildir. Önce o karenin
muhtemel kötü üretimi tahayyül edilir; sonra yalnız o kareyi bozan somut
sapmalar engellenir. Bir sonraki kareye otomatik taşınmaz.

MOTION İÇİN ŞİMDİDEN KİLİT
Kalem ve defter görünse bile hiç kimse okunabilir yazı yazmaz. İleride el
hareketi gerekirse yalnız anlamsız karalama veya çizgi izi oluşur.

### K01 | VO1
### “Bu Efe; altıncı sınıfa gidiyor ve okula her sabah yürüyerek gider.”
### yazı: YOK
#########################################################################

FİKİR: Uzun metraj filmin açılış karesi. Efe’yi adı değil, her sabah geçtiği
sokağın canlı ritmi içindeki kendine özgü yürüyüşü tanıtır.

PLAN: Sokağın uzun perspektifi değil, yağmur oluğundan sıçrayan sabah ışığının
çizdiği dar şerit baskın geometridir. Efe bu ışık şeridini çapraz keser. Ön
plandaki buğulu otobüs camı kadrajın üçte birini örter; şehir hazır dekor değil,
gözlenen gerçek bir yer gibi hissedilir.

PROMPT:
High-end cinematic feature-animation 3D still with the dimensional lighting,
character appeal and finish of a theatrical animated film. A 40mm lens at a
sixth-grader’s shoulder height looks through the
fogged lower corner of a city-bus shelter; the translucent glass occupies the
entire left third of frame and catches soft fingerprints and a pale reflection
of the waking street. Beyond it, @efe crosses a narrow blade of clean morning
sun on his walk to school, caught between two steps, satchel pulling one
shoulder slightly lower. He is not presenting himself and never looks at the
camera. His small worn notebook remains tucked in the bib pocket, only its
rounded cover visible.

The Turkish neighbourhood feels lavishly built and alive without competing
with him: stacked ochre and faded-rose façades catch first sun, balcony laundry
turns translucent, a simit cart rolls partly out of frame, rainwater glitters
in the gutter, and a cobalt school bus recedes into pearly haze. Foreground
glass and shadow simplify this richness around Efe instead of removing it.
A vivid petrol-blue patch on his clothing, copper sesame crusts, coral façades
and a thin lemon-white sun edge form a luminous colour chord. Surfaces are
tactile—damp stone, worn denim, fogged glass, brushed metal—while skin and eyes
carry sophisticated subsurface light. Efe’s face receives a brief soft
reflection from the shelter, thoughtful and alive, mouth closed. Asymmetrical
blockbuster composition, graceful foreground occlusion, deep cinematic
atmosphere, frozen at the most appealing instant of premium character acting.

Three movement beats belong to this exact morning: the loose satchel strap
finishes a backward swing, condensation gathers into one slow bead on the
shelter glass, and the departing bus exhales a faint ribbon into the haze.
Depth in three layers—finger-marked shelter glass large and soft in the near
plane, @efe sharp in the middle plane, bus and shutter dissolving in the far
haze. His planted shoe rests in contact with the paving and compresses slightly
against it; the satchel has visible downward weight and nothing floats.

STYLE: theatrical high-end feature-animation 3D; expressive and appealing
pre-teen proportions, sophisticated warm matte tan skin with low specular and
no green or grey cast, luminous eyes, rich tactile materials, lush environmental
world-building under strict focal hierarchy, motivated natural light,
volumetric depth, elegant vivid colour separation and continuous physically
based shading.

TEXT: No readable text. The notebook is closed; the bus and shops carry no
legible markings.

NEGATIVE: do not turn Efe toward the lens or place him in a centred hero pose;
do not show his full body cleanly from head to toe like a character sheet; do
not fill the pavement with evenly spaced bins, lamps, bicycles and shop props;
do not make the shelter glass crystal-clear or remove its fog and fingerprints;
do not turn the street into a glossy miniature set; no name label saying EFE;
no readable notebook page, bus route, shop sign or licence plate.

### K02 | VO2
### “O sabah köşedeki kaldırım rampasının önünde bir araba duruyordu.”
### yazı: YOK
#########################################################################

FİKİR: Araba yalnız yanlış park etmiş bir araç değil; açık renkli yaya yolunu
tek hamlede kesen ağır, koyu bir kütle.

PLAN: Neredeyse kuşbakışı, büyük bir animasyon filminin cesur vinç kadrajı. Soluk beton
rampa kadrajı çapraz geçen bir ok gibi başlar; koyu vişne renkli otomobil onu
tam ortasında keser. İnsan yüzü gerekmiyor; düşüncesizlik, aceleyle kapanan
kapıda ve hâlâ sallanan anahtarlıkta görülür.

PROMPT:
High-end theatrical feature-animation 3D with bold visual storytelling and
vivid production design. A 28mm lens cranes from a second-floor balcony over
@kose, looking steeply down without
becoming a flat diagram. The pale dropped-kerb ramp enters from the lower left
as a strong diagonal path and should have continued toward the upper right,
but a dark sour-cherry compact car lies across it like a heavy bar. Its front
tyre has climbed onto the tactile grooves; the compressed rubber and the
broken line of the ramp are the sharpest details in frame.

Only the driver’s coat hem, bread bag and hand are visible at the extreme upper
edge as he pulls a shop door closed; his keys hang low and catch one hard spark
of sun. This is unthinking haste, not villainy. A gust has pushed three dry
plane leaves against the trapped edge of the ramp. Everything else falls away:
chalky pavement, one tree-shadow, the car roof carrying a soft rectangle of
sky. Colour is built from sun-warmed ivory concrete, deep sour-cherry paint,
violet-blue cast shadow, fresh green plane leaves and one glowing kraft-paper
accent. Balcony flowers add tiny distant colour notes without stealing focus.
The car paint carries pearlescent depth beneath road dust and a dull roof,
never showroom gloss. Strong graphic readability comes from real objects,
theatrical lighting and scale—not arrows or labels. Every surface has the
dimensional finish and material richness of a premium animated feature.

Three movement beats belong to the interruption: keys finish a small swing at
the upper edge, three trapped plane leaves scrape against the ramp, and a last
veil of warm engine shimmer thins behind the car. Depth in three layers—the
chipped ramp lip cuts the near plane, tyre and broken path are sharp in the
middle, driver fragments and shop door fall soft at the far edge.
The compressed tyre and car chassis remain visibly anchored to the sloped
concrete with firm contact shadows; nothing floats.

STYLE: theatrical high-end feature-animation 3D; bold appeal-driven geometry,
rich but controlled neighbourhood world-building, luminous vivid colour,
weather-softened tactile materials, motivated morning sun, sculpted bounce
light, volumetric atmosphere, continuous physically based shading and
unmistakable feature-film scale rather than miniature-diorama sheen.

TEXT: No readable text on any surface.

NEGATIVE: do not place the car neatly beside the ramp or leave a usable gap;
do not make the ramp disappear completely beneath the car—the exposed tactile
grooves must prove what is blocked; do not show an angry, guilty or threatening
driver; do not turn the overhead view into a flat map, infographic or perfect
top-down orthographic image; do not make the car pristine, oversized like a toy,
or brightly heroic; no road arrows, accessibility icon, warning sign, licence
plate, badge or shop lettering.

Use this referenced image, K02, and change ONLY the human action: preserve the

Catch him mid-step with his shoulders naturally counter-rotated, one hand
briefly near the car body for balance but not touching it, eyes fixed on the
open street ahead. Remove the driver fragments and bread bag. The blocked
ramp remains clearly visible below and behind Efe, but he does not notice it.
Keep the moment cinematic, asymmetrical and physically weighted.

TEXT: No readable text.

NEGATIVE: do not make Efe climb over the ramp, squeeze between car and kerb,

### K04 | VO4
### “Arkasından gelen sınıf arkadaşı Derin ise dolanamadı.”
### yazı: YOK
#########################################################################

FİKİR: Bir santimetrelik fiziksel gerçek, bütün sokağın soyut “erişilebilirlik”
lafından daha güçlü. Kaldırım taşı kadrajda duvar gibi yükselir.

PLAN: Kamera Derin’in diz hizasında, kaldırımın hemen karşısında. Ön teker ile
taş arasındaki ince gölge yarığı baskın odak. Derin’in yüzü krom el halkasının
eğri yansımasında değil, sağ üstte kısmen görünür; insan kalır, sandalye sergi
nesnesine dönüşmez.

PROMPT:
High-end vivid feature-animation close-up from ground level, using a 65mm lens
placed just beyond the granite kerb at @kose. The kerb rises across the lower
half of the frame like a weathered stone wall. On the far side, @derin’s small
front castor has stopped a finger-width before it; that narrow dark seam is the
sharpest and most important mark in the image. Her near rear wheel enters as a
large arc from the right edge, with her hand bearing real weight on the chrome
push-rim—palm against the rim, thumb following the upper edge, fingers curled
beneath.

Derin leans forward from the shoulder with contained effort. Only part of her
face appears in the upper-right quadrant, framed between wheel and car body:
eyes measuring the kerb, jaw set, mouth closed; neither sadness nor performance.
The blocked ramp and car are present only as soft, recognisable shapes further
along the pavement. Light is cooler here: open-sky blue on the stone and chair,
with a fierce warm reflection running around one short section of chrome and
catching her cheek. Grit, road dust and tiny chips give scale. The image should
feel like the emotionally precise turning point of a theatrical animated film,
captured at the exact instant movement became impossible—premium character
appeal, bold depth, sophisticated facial restraint and no sentimental poster
illustration.

Three movement beats carry only her effort: road dust is still settling beside
the stopped castor, one escaped hair crosses her cheek, and the short warm glint
on the chrome rim tightens under her grip. Depth in three layers—the chipped
kerb face dominates the near plane, castor, hand and push-rim are sharp in the
middle, Derin’s partial face and the blocked car recede softly behind. Both
tyres rest in contact with the street, carrying her weight through compressed
contact patches and grounded shadows; no component floats.

STYLE: theatrical high-end feature-animation 3D; appealing restrained pre-teen
acting, sophisticated warm matte tan skin with low specular and no green or grey
cast, luminous expressive eyes, believable manual-wheelchair mechanics, vivid
sculpted daylight, tactile street materials, cinematic focus falloff and rich
feature-film dimensionality without medical-display polish.

TEXT: No readable text.

NEGATIVE: do not let the castor climb, touch or float above the kerb; do not
widen the gap into an easy-to-cross space; do not detach, duplicate or melt
Derin’s fingers into the push-rim; do not turn the wheelchair into hospital
equipment with straps, headrest, clinical white parts or oversized handles;
do not make Derin cry, lower her head, smile bravely at camera or appear helpless;
do not hide the blocked car so completely that the cause becomes mysterious;
no accessibility symbol, caption, arrow or pity-poster composition.

### K06 | VO6
### “Efe o sabah, her gün yanından geçtiği bir şeyi ilk kez gördü.”
### yazı: YOK
#########################################################################

FİKİR: Efe, Derin’i doğrudan değil önce otomobil aynasında görür. Gündelik
nesne bir anda tanıklık aracına dönüşür; “ilk kez görmek” görsel olarak olur.

PLAN: Ayna kadrajın merkezinde küçük ama keskin bir pencere. Aynanın içinde
Derin ve kapalı rampa; aynanın dışında Efe’nin yakın yüzü ve arabanın dev,
bulanık gövdesi. İki mekân değil, aynı sokağın tek optik yansıması.

PROMPT:
High-end theatrical feature-animation 3D at its most cinematic and emotionally
precise, 85mm lens at shallow depth of field beside the parked sour-cherry car
at @kose. The car’s dusty wing
mirror sits just left of centre, small but razor sharp. Inside its correctly
oriented reflection, @derin is stopped at the kerb with one hand on her
push-rim; the pale ramp visibly ends beneath the same parked car. The reflection
is compressed but physically plausible, the visual heart of the reveal.

Outside the mirror, @efe occupies the near right edge in close three-quarter
profile, partly cropped by frame. He has just turned back. His face is softly
out of focus except for the near eye, whose gaze lands precisely on the mirror;
his mouth stays closed and his expression is the quiet jolt of recognition,
not theatrical shock. The car body becomes a broad sour-cherry field separating
him from the reflected scene. A narrow band of white morning sky crosses the
mirror, a muted blue shadow wraps Efe’s cheek, and one warm glint joins his eye
to the chrome rim inside the reflection. Dust on glass, fine scratches on mirror
housing and road grit prevent polished toy perfection. The sour-cherry body,
petrol-blue clothing, ivory sky band and warm chrome spark form a vivid
four-note palette. Bold feature-film framing, foreground obstruction, selective
clarity, luminous eye acting and emotionally restrained premium 3D.

Three movement beats sharpen recognition without spectacle: Efe’s near eye
finishes its turn toward the mirror, a dust mote crosses the white sky-band in
the reflection, and the reflected chrome rim gives back one brief warm glint.
Depth in three layers—the sour-cherry door and Efe’s cropped cheek occupy the
soft near plane, the dusty mirror is razor sharp in the middle, Derin and the
ramp remain compressed inside its far reflection. The mirror housing rests in contact
with the car door through a firm mounting seam, and every wheel in the
reflection meets the street with plausible contact; nothing floats.

STYLE: theatrical high-end feature-animation 3D; sophisticated warm matte tan
skin with low specular and no green or grey cast, luminous expressive eye
shading, optically plausible reflection, richly weathered surfaces, vivid
colour separation, sculpted morning light, shallow cinematic focus and
emotionally precise feature-film acting without showroom polish.

TEXT: No readable text.

NEGATIVE: do not place Derin physically inside or behind the mirror housing;
do not create a portal, split screen, inset panel or second floating image;
do not duplicate Derin outside the reflection; do not mirror her wheelchair
into impossible geometry or reverse the street layout; do not make Efe stare
at the lens, gape, point dramatically or produce a cartoon shock face; do not
make every object equally sharp; do not clean the dust and scratches from the
mirror; no thought bubble, written moral, accessibility icon or caption.
