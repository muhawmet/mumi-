>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

A warm, unhurried orchestral underscore for a children's science film set in a village
garden at early morning — a hen sitting on her eggs, kittens asleep in the barn corner,
a shallow pond further down the slope. The feeling is patient attention: something is
quietly happening inside things that look still. Small woodwind figures carry curiosity,
low strings hold the ground, a single soft celesta note marks each small discovery.
The middle opens outward for the pond, then draws back close and tender for the nest.
Near the end one long sustained string swell for the transformation, then the theme
returns exactly as it began — same key, same tempo — because we end where we started.
No drums kit, no percussion hits, no electronic elements, no vocals, no lyrics.
Nothing triumphant, nothing sad, nothing tense.

KURGUDA KULLANIM

· K09-K22 (gölet)           : açılım — burada müzik en geniş yerindedir
· K23-K46 (kümes/ahır/üç yol): orta yoğunluk, celesta noktaları kavram anlarına denk gelir
· K47-K52 (başkalaşım)      : uzun yaylı yükseliş — dersin fikri burada
· K53-K54 (ayna kapanış)    : açılışın teması birebir döner, aynı tonda, aynı tempoda
