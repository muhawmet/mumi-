K01 — dört tek-nesne düzeltmesi (kompozisyon KORUNUR)
=========================================================================
Kare iyi: pervazdaki 2023/2021/2019 kusursuz, saat, çaydanlık, raflar, palto, aydınlık
neşeli oda — hepsi kalıyor. Dört şey değişiyor.

-----
Use this image as the reference and keep everything in it — the same room, the same camera,
the same light, the same boy, the same clock, kettle, shelves, coat and the same pencil
years on the door jamb. Change only these four things.

The boy steps back out of the kitchen and stands in the doorway itself, on the corridor
side of the threshold, both feet behind the line of the door frame; his right palm rests
flat on the wooden jamb at his own shoulder height with his thumb touching the topmost
pencil line, and his other arm hangs relaxed at his side. Nothing of him is inside the
kitchen and neither hand reaches toward anything in the room.

Where the knife block now stands on the counter there is instead a shallow white enamel
bowl with a soft drift of flour beside it, and the counter carries nothing else.

The oven below the cooker is closed and cold — its glass door is dark and quiet, with the
same warm room light simply falling across it; the warm glow in the lower right of the
frame comes only from the small iron stove vent, not from inside any appliance.

The window behind the boy is completely fogged over, edge to edge, an even matte pane that
glows bright white with the winter light behind it — nothing outside is visible through it,
no tree, no branch, no snow shape, no garden, only clean bright fog.

And on the table at the right, the thin-waisted tea glass stands upside down, resting on
its own rim, empty and dry.
-----

Keep all of that identical. Change only three things, and nothing else.

The water in the upper brewing pot is now deep amber, dark at the bottom and thinning
upward, filling the glass body from base to shoulder.

The tea glass on the table now stands the right way up, filled to its brim with the same
deep amber tea.
