### K01 | 5s · VO "Efe kış sabahı mutfağın kapısında durdu."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kapı boşluğu sabit bir çerçeve olarak dursun, kamera pervazın arkasından
bir adım kadar içeri sızsın ve mutfağı Efe'nin durduğu yerden göstersin.
-----
🔴 MOTION INTENT: Efe pervazda kalır ve hiçbir şeye uzanmaz; mutfağın beş işi onsuz
dönerken yalnız sarkaç, buhar ipliği ve camdaki damla oynar, klip bitiminde odada
değişen tek şey damlanın indiği mesafedir.
-----
DURUM: 🔴 YENİDEN-BASIM — Efe kapıda değil ocağın yanında ve açık avucu kaynayan
çaydanlığın buharının içinde; VO'yu duymayan izleyici için kare iki türlü okunuyor.

### K02 | 5s · VO "Ocakta çaydanlık ısınıyor, duvardaki saat işliyordu."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kamera ÇAY kutusunun önünden geçerek kaysın — mesafe hiç değişmesin ki
harf düzlemde kalsın, ama paralaks çaydanlığı kutudan ayırsın ve oda derinlik kazansın.
frame: images/2.png · sha256:26e0787e6a4fb495
-----
Heat is already in the metal when the shot begins: a fine chain of bubbles keeps letting go
of the inner wall and climbing, while a thin thread of steam leans off the spout toward the
shelf. Then one larger bubble breaks free and the thread thickens — and at that instant the
brass pendulum behind reaches the end of its return and starts back, two speeds running
side by side without either noticing the other. The clip begins with a kettle merely
warming and ends with a kettle plainly working, while the clock keeps the same patient beat
through both. The room holds as it is: the pane burning white at the right, the jars still
on their shelf, the blue ring steady underneath. Camera: from hob height the frame drifts a
hand's width left so the caddy passes in front of the kettle with real parallax, holding
the same distance throughout, and it is still easing when the clip ends. The water in the
upper glass pot stays completely colourless from first frame to last, and the ÇAY letters
keep their shape, spacing and depth on a caddy that never lifts, tips or slides.

### K03 | 5s · VO "Cam buğulanmış, sobanın üstünde süt tenceresi duruyordu."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kamera tencerenin yanından rafın hizasına doğru yükselsin, kediyi
sonda bulsun — ama SÜT harfi düzlemde kalsın diye mesafe değişmesin.
-----
🔴 MOTION INTENT: Süt kabarmadan yüzeydeki kaymak bir tık genişler, kedinin kulağı sesin
yönüne döner ve camdaki damla kendi izinde bir parmak daha iner — üç iş, üç ayrı hız,
hiçbiri ötekine değmez.
-----
DURUM: 🔴 YENİDEN-BASIM — kavanoz omuzlarında İngilizce hece parçaları ("AR", "ER")
okunuyor ve tencerenin altında gaz yerine kızıl elektrikli rezistans halkası yanıyor.

### K04 | 5s · VO "Onlarca iş aynı anda oluyor, hiçbiri çarpışmıyordu."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Tek bir yavaş yanal kayış altı işi sırayla kadrajdan geçirsin — kesim
yapmadan, hiçbirinin üstünde durmadan; eşzamanlılığı kameranın kendisi kanıtlasın.
-----
🔴 MOTION INTENT: Altı yarım iş altı ayrı yerde kendi hızında bir adım ilerler ve klip
biterken altısı da hâlâ yarımdır, hiçbiri ötekine değmemiştir.
-----
DURUM: 🔴 YENİDEN-BASIM — mutfağı tanımlayan geniş kare loş (KİLİT 4ø) ve karenin kendi
yasakladığı bıçaklık odakta duruyor.

### K05 | 5s · VO "Efe'nin vücudunda da tam o anda onlarca iş sürüyordu."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kamera sabit kalır ve hiç yaklaşmaz — bu karenin fikri "aynı anda çok iş",
ve eşzamanlılık ancak kadraj bütünken okunur; kamera bir işe yaklaşırsa diğerlerini siler.
frame: images/5.png · sha256:75b0ffedb0dc18f4
-----
Nobody is in the kitchen and five separate works are running at once. A single thin thread
of steam leaves the upper brewing pot and leans slowly toward the window, thinning as it
goes. On the milk pot beside it a skin has begun to set at the rim, and one small dome
lifts under that skin, holds, and settles back flat. The clock's pendulum swings out to
the far end of its travel, hangs there for a breath and starts back, its brass edge picking
up the window light each time it passes. On the fogged pane a single bead of condensation
breaks loose and runs a short way down the glass, leaving a clear wet track above it while
the fog below stays untouched. And on the dark stone counter one grain of flour lifts off
the edge, turns once in the air and settles again. Nothing waits for anything else — the
five run on their own separate clocks and none of them finishes; the room simply keeps
working. Camera: locked off at counter height, square to the run of the worktop, absolutely
still so all five works stay inside the frame at once. Silent clip, no audio, no dialogue,
mouth closed, no lip movement. No whip-pan, no shake, no snap-zoom, no camera warp.

### K06 | 5s · VO "Kalbi atıyor, ciğerleri nefes alıyor, kasları çalışıyordu."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kamera pencerenin yanından ona doğru yürüsün ve yükselsin — nefes
sütununu ışığın içinden geçirerek göstermenin tek yolu bu açıdan gelmek.
frame: images/6.png · sha256:e8055f8254c3f6c5
-----
Breath leaves him in one slow column as the clip runs: the plume standing in the cold air
before his face keeps unrolling and thinning, and under his left palm the knitted cloth
lifts and falls with it. Then the column reaches as far as it will go and begins to lose
itself — and as it goes his chin lowers a little and the four tendons on the back of his
right hand ease where his weight rests on the counter. The clip begins with a body plainly
at work and ends with the same body a shade calmer, and no part of it has been opened to
explain why. The kitchen holds around him: the fogged pane filling the left as a bright
field, the flour still on the dark stone. Camera: from his left the frame travels slowly
toward him with the window frame passing through the near foreground, rising a few
centimetres as it comes, and it is still moving when the clip ends. His lips stay exactly as
the frame has them, parted only for breathing, and never move into a speech shape; his face
holds its features; the breath stays real vapour with a soft dissipating end and never
becomes a ribbon, line or drawn arrow.
Silent clip, no audio, no dialogue, no lip movement, jaw held.

### K07 | 5s · VO "Peki bütün bunları aynı anda kim yönetiyordu?"
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kamera onun bakış yönüne doğru bir yay çizsin — soru sözle değil, kameranın
elsiz çalışan işlere doğru dönmesiyle sorulsun.
frame: images/7.png · sha256:9963cf7c7d64d3c2
-----
Up on the shelf his fingers still rest on the rim of the jar he has just pushed back into
line, and the jar rocks down its last hair onto its foot while behind him four other works
run with nobody near them. Then the jar settles for good under his fingertips — and at that
moment his head, already turned over his shoulder, comes round the last few degrees and his
eyes travel to the far side of the kitchen, where the steam, the pendulum and the halted
bead go on without him. He begins the clip having just finished one small job and ends it
looking for whoever is doing all the others. The room keeps its own light: the corridor a
soft blue rectangle at the left, the pane glowing at the right. Camera: from below the shelf
line the frame arcs a few degrees around him toward his eyeline and rises with the turn of
his head, and it is still travelling when the clip ends. His hand stays on the jar rim and
the jar never lifts, tips or leaves the shelf; his face holds its features and his lips stay
closed and soft.
Silent clip, no audio, no dialogue, mouth closed, no lip movement.

### K08 | 5s · VO "Cevap iki sistemdeydi: sinir sistemi ve iç salgı bezleri."
KAMERA NİYETİ: Kamera tek eksende, alttaki metalden üstteki cama doğru yükselsin — dersin
iki hızı kameranın kat ettiği mesafeyle üst üste okunsun.
frame: images/8.png · sha256:e9f52c8d3e6e6cce
-----
One flame feeds both vessels and the difference is already running as the shot opens:
below, the water turns over hard against the inner wall and the steam leaves the spout in a
tight hard-edged jet; above, in the glass pot, the amber lies deep at the bottom and lifts
one slow thread that loses itself with no edge. Then the boil gathers and the jet thickens
— and while that happens the amber only reaches a little further into the water it is
already inside, going nowhere. The clip begins with two vessels on one ring and ends with
the same two, one faster and one wider, which is the whole lesson said without a word. The
kitchen stays where it is: the pane burning white behind, the ring steady underneath.
Camera: from just above hob height the frame rises slowly along the stacked column, staying
on one axis and holding the same distance from the glass, and it is still rising when the
clip ends. The amber stays inside the water, never reaches the top of the pot and never
becomes a beam, halo or light touching anything else; the metal holds its rigid solid form
with no deform, melt, morph or merge.
Silent clip, no audio, no dialogue, mouth closed, no lip movement. No whip-pan, no shake, no snap-zoom, no camera warp.
