>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

A tender, patient orchestral underscore for a children's science film about a flower, a
bee and a bean seed — the feeling of a slow spring morning on a balcony where something
very small is quietly working. Opens with warm nylon-string guitar and a single sustained
cello note, unhurried, almost still; a soft flute line drifts in like the first sun. A
gentle harp figure carries the middle of the film, circling and returning, patient rather
than pretty. Strings gather slowly and warmly at the emotional centre, one long rising
line that resolves without ever becoming grand. The last section is the quietest and the
most hopeful: solo piano over held low strings, and the nylon guitar from the opening
comes back one last time. Tempo slow and breathing, around 72 BPM, gentle 4/4 with no
drums beyond an occasional soft mallet. Plenty of room for a narrator — the mid-range is
deliberately kept clear and nothing competes with a speaking voice. Instrumental only,
no vocals, no lyrics.
