· >>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<
· --------------------------------------------------------------------

A clean modern minimalist underscore for a premium children's science film about a flower, a bee and a bean seed. Felt piano leads: a short repeating four-note motif, close-mic'd and intimate, with the felt dampers and the soft mechanical action of the keys audible. Underneath it a warm analog synth pad breathes slowly in and out, and a very quiet arpeggiated electronic pulse keeps time without ever becoming a beat. The whole piece is built on space and patience rather than melody — think contemporary Scandinavian neoclassical, the sound of a modern product film, elegant and uncluttered. It grows only twice: once in the middle where a low sustained synth bass and a single sustained cello join underneath and the piano motif opens up a fourth, and once near the end where the pad swells warmly and resolves upward. Everything else stays sparse. Tempo unhurried, around 76 BPM, gentle 4/4, no drum kit — only a soft filtered pulse and the occasional low sub note. The mid-range is deliberately kept clear so a narrator sits on top of it with nothing competing. The final twenty seconds thin out to the felt piano motif alone over one held pad note, and the last four seconds fade slowly and completely to silence. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
