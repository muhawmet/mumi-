>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

A warm, curious orchestral-and-piano underscore for a children's science film about
discovering something impossibly small — the feeling of a kid leaning into a microscope
for the first time. Begins sparse and domestic: single felted upright piano notes, soft
pizzicato strings, a slow warm cello line underneath, like a quiet morning in a garden.
Around the one-minute mark a gentle marimba and celesta pattern enters and starts to
tick along, small and precise and mechanical, the sound of tiny things working. Strings
open into a warm, wondering swell in the middle section, never triumphant, always
intimate. Light glass-harmonica shimmer appears sparingly for the moments of discovery.
Returns at the end to the same solo piano it opened with, one octave higher, settling.
Tempo unhurried, around 80 BPM, gentle 4/4, no drums beyond a soft brushed pulse.
Plenty of room for a narrator — the mid-range is deliberately kept clear and nothing
competes with a speaking voice. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
