24mm lens at f/5.6, elevated wide view, deep focus — the composition, framing, camera height, character pose and lighting are taken from the reference image exactly; the neighbourhood itself is rebuilt. A luminous cool-blue straight beam of light spears from @mira's home, small on the left of the frame, straight across the whole width of the picture to her school far away on the right, the shortest possible line, cutting cleanly across the faint warm-red winding ribbon still tracing the streets below; the home and the school each glow softly as an endpoint node. The straight blue beam is the hero of the frame — an in-scene glowing light object, a clean directional streak with no arrowhead and no diagram. @mira stands small on a flat roof terrace in the lower left, both feet flat and well back from the edge behind a chest-height glass-and-slim-steel guard railing, watching the beam form. Warm matte tan skin, never tinted green or grey, a single soft catchlight in the eyes only and never a wet sheen over the face — skin stays matte with low specular. The setting is a modern, well-kept Turkish new-development district seen from above: rows of low three-to-five storey contemporary apartment blocks in clean warm off-white and light-grey render, flat or barely pitched roofs finished in pale grey membrane with neat parapets and clear uncluttered surfaces, large windows and glass-and-slim-steel balcony railings; wide, freshly laid smooth dark-grey asphalt streets with crisp white lane lines curving between them; light-grey concrete-paver pavements with granite kerbs, mown grass strips, young trees in round grated beds and low clipped hedges; slim grey lamp posts and a few clean new compact cars parked neatly. Far away on the right the school reads unmistakably as a school: a contemporary two-storey school building with clean horizontal lines, long ribbons of large windows, coloured facade panels in warm amber and soft blue, a straight flagpole on its roof flying the Turkish flag — a red field with a white crescent and a white five-pointed star — and a legible Turkish sign board above its main entrance. Three things are alive in the frame: the blue beam's cool light grazes the pale roof parapets, the two endpoint nodes softly pulse, a small flock of birds crosses the clear morning air. Depth in three layers — a blurred roof parapet edge in the near foreground, the blue beam sharp across the mid-ground, the far end of the district cooling into clean atmospheric perspective. Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow.
STYLE: Full 3D CGI feature-animation render in the premium-CG feature-animation lineage — appeal-driven composition that reads at thumbnail size, subsurface translucency where skin appears, a single soft catchlight in the eyes only and never a wet sheen over the face; skin stays matte with low specular. Structures drawn with clear child-readable scale. Lighting is art-directed: the low morning key plus the blue beam as a second motivated glowing source casting cool bounce, soft painted ambient occlusion — shadow reads open and detailed, never cold-black. No outlines; forms carry through lighting and value.

Render as light behaviour, never flat colour fills. No muddy midtone, no teal-orange grade, no noir contrast.
