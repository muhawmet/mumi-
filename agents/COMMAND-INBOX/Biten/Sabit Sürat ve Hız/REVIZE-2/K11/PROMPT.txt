Depth in three layers — the bright near end of the ribbon in the foreground, its curving body sharp in the mid-ground, the far street in warm bokeh. Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow.
STYLE: Full 3D CGI feature-animation render in the premium-CG feature-animation lineage — appeal-driven silhouette that reads at thumbnail size, subsurface translucency where skin appears, a single soft catchlight in the eyes only and never a wet sheen over the face; skin stays matte with low specular.
