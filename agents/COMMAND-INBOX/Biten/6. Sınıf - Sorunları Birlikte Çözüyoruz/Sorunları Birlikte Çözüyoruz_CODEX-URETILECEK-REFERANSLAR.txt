### REFERANS 1 — @kose
### Türk mahallesi sokak köşesi · ana hikâye mekânı
#########################################################################

35mm lens at f/5.6, eye-level wide three-quarter master view of one specific
street corner in a Turkish neighbourhood, composed as a high-end theatrical
feature-animation environment reference. The dropped-kerb pedestrian ramp is
the unmistakable anchor: pale warm-grey concrete cutting diagonally through
the granite kerb in the near third, two shallow tactile grooves crossing its
surface, one chipped corner exposing small aggregate. The ramp is completely
open and readable.

A mature plane tree rises beside it from a square of dark soil inside a low
weathered iron guard. Behind it, four-storey apartment buildings step around
the corner in warm ochre, faded coral and dusty rose lime render. Their balconies
have deep green iron rails, translucent laundry, terracotta flowerpots, irregular
air-conditioning units and small satellite dishes. Ground-floor shops are
closed behind aged corrugated shutters; fabric awnings are rolled and tied.
The façades are richly built and inhabited, never a blank architectural shell.

Street furniture is memorable but naturally placed: one deep-green municipal
bin with its lid shut, one fluted cast-iron lamp, one red-brown post box, a
bus-stop pole whose glass case reflects only sky, and one worn bicycle locked
to the tree guard. The road carries a slightly sunken manhole cover, faded
crossing paint and a narrow rain gutter. No people and no vehicles.

Early-morning light enters from the street opening at left, laying a broad
warm-golden band across the ramp and lower façades while cool lavender-blue
open-sky bounce shapes the shaded walls. Balcony flowers, green rails and the
coral render create vivid colour through real materials and light—not flat
saturation. Fine haze catches between the buildings; leaf translucency and
soft atmospheric depth give the scale of a premium animated feature. Concrete,
granite, peeling lime render, oxidised iron, rubber and bark are materially
distinct. Continuous physically based feature-animation shading, elegant
appeal-driven forms, no universal prop overscale.

TEXT: No readable text. Shutters have no shop names; the timetable case returns
only sky.

NEGATIVE: do not put a car, person or moving bicycle on the ramp; do not widen,
relocate or hide the ramp; do not turn the corner into a spotless European
tourist street, a suburban avenue or a flat architectural visualisation; do not
make every building identical in colour or height; do not make the street props
toy-sized, glossy or evenly spaced; no shop lettering, road sign, licence plate,
logo or English text.

### REFERANS 2 — @sinif
### Altıncı sınıf dersliği · çözüm toplantısı mekânı
#########################################################################

32mm lens at f/5.6, standing eye height from the back-left corner of one specific
Turkish sixth-grade classroom, a high-end theatrical feature-animation
environment reference with vivid lived-in production design. The room has
three staggered rows of scratched satin-varnish timber desks on dark tubular
steel frames, chairs pushed in at slightly different angles, leaving a clear
central path toward the front wall.

The front wall is anchored by one large modern interactive smart board mounted
flat and switched completely off, its dark graphite screen reflecting only a
soft rectangle of window light. A narrow rail beneath it holds two capped
markers and one felt eraser. Beside it stands a teacher's desk with a stack of
dog-eared exercise books, a ceramic pencil cup, a stapler and a small potted
plant leaning toward the windows.

The long window wall is alive with high-end feature-film detail: tall panes,
one slightly crooked cream blind, sun-warmed dust suspended in the light,
a painted radiator with one knitted glove drying on it, and a deep teal curtain
gathered at the end. The opposite wall is already dressed with overlapping
children's paintings, a rolled wall map tied shut, a cork strip layered with
papers turned away from the lens, low shelves of box files and handmade clay
objects. Coat hooks by the door carry two school bags and one forgotten rust-
orange jacket. Nothing looks arranged for a catalogue.

Clear late-morning sun falls in broad soft bands across the desks, turning wood
honey-gold and leaving sculpted violet-blue shade beneath the chair frames.
Accents of teal curtain, coral jacket, yellow pencil cup and children's artwork
make the room vivid without becoming rainbow noise. Chalky painted walls,
worn wood grain, powder-coated metal, woven fabric and dark screen glass remain
materially distinct. Deep three-layer space, gentle volumetric dust, continuous
physically based feature-animation shading, rich theatrical animation finish.
No people.

TEXT: No readable text. The board is off; paintings and papers contain only
soft non-semantic shapes.

NEGATIVE: do not replace the smart board with a blackboard or chalkboard; do not
turn on the screen or invent an interface; do not make the classroom empty,
sterile, symmetrical or newly furnished; do not align every chair and desk
perfectly; do not fill the walls with legible posters or random English words;
do not make the room photorealistic, flat-lit, miniature-like or plastic-glossy;
no students, teacher, school logo or institutional name.

### REFERANS 3 — @kutu
### Üçlü ayrıştırılmış atık kutusu · tekrar eden hero-prop
#########################################################################

70mm lens at f/5.6, waist-height three-quarter product-character view of one
connected set of three municipal separated-waste bins, rendered as a high-end
theatrical feature-animation hero prop rather than a sterile catalogue object.
All three bins share exactly the same moulded body, height and lid geometry and
stand side by side in one weathered galvanised-steel cradle.

Left bin: deep vivid blue, with a narrow paper slot in its lid. Middle bin:
warm ochre-brown, with a round glass-bottle opening. Right bin: muted rich green,
with one broad plastic opening. Their polyethylene surfaces have fine mould
grain, soft chalking from sun, pale scuff arcs beneath the lids and small dark
handling marks around the apertures. The shared steel cradle has a dull spangled
finish, one slightly bent foot plate and a short chain fixed to a ground anchor.
All feet rest firmly on worn stone paving with convincing contact shadows.

On each front, one hand-stencilled Turkish word is painted directly into the
plastic grain in matte black. Blue reads "KAĞIT" — five capital letters,
K-A-Ğ-I-T, with a breve above Ğ and no dot above the capital dotless I. Brown
reads "CAM" — three capital letters, C-A-M. Green reads "PLASTİK" — seven capital
letters, P-L-A-S-T-İ-K, with a dot above the capital İ. Each word reads normally
left-to-right, one word per bin, large and unobstructed.

The set stands in a small paved schoolyard service corner rather than a blank
studio: a coral wall soft in the distance, one band of cobalt shadow, a few dry
plane leaves near the cradle and warm late-afternoon light grazing the plastic
grain. Blue, ochre and green remain rich but materially believable. Continuous
physically based feature-animation shading, dimensional lid edges, tactile
plastic and metal, premium animated-feature finish.

TEXT: Only the three exact Turkish words "KAĞIT", "CAM" and "PLASTİK".

NEGATIVE: do not generate more or fewer than three bins; do not change their
shared body shape or relative size; do not swap the blue, brown and green order;
do not add recycling arrows, triangular symbols, pictograms, barcodes or brand
marks; do not misspell, mirror, duplicate or split any of the three words; do
not add a dot above the dotless I in "KAĞIT" or remove the dot above İ in
"PLASTİK"; do not make the bins pristine, translucent, wet, toy-like or
high-gloss; no extra label, fourth colour or loose detached lid.
