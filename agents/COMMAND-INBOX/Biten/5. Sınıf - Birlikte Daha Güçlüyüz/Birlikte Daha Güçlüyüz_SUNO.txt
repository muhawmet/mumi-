>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

A warm, patient orchestral-acoustic underscore for a children's documentary about a neighbourhood rebuilding itself after a flood — it opens grey and uncertain with a single soft felt piano and a low sustained cello, then gathers as more instruments join one at a time, the way neighbours arrive one by one, until a full warm string section and gentle acoustic guitar are carrying it together; mid-piece it steps back into something older and quieter, a lone plucked stringed instrument with a slight Anatolian modal colour, unhurried and dignified, before the strings return and lift into a hopeful, unforced resolution with soft mallet percussion and a light rhythmic pulse like hands working in time; tempo slow to moderate around eighty beats per minute, never triumphant, never sentimental, never epic trailer music; dynamics kept low and even with plenty of room for a narrator throughout, nothing competing in the midrange; instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics, no choir, no vocal pads.
