5. SÜRTÜNME — MÜZİK (Suno v5.5) · ENSTRÜMANTAL FON SKORU
=========================================================================
Amaç: 5. sınıf fen anlatısının ALTINDA yürüyen sıcak, meraklı, oynak-eğitici skor.
VO önde; müzik -18 dB civarı, VO'yu boğmaz. Vokal YOK (ses = ElevenLabs VO ayrı).
Hedef süre ~2:40 (Suno tek parça ~2-4dk verir; gerekirse "Extend" ile uzat, sonra
Premiere'de edit-plan'daki sekans enerjilerine göre kırp/yerleştir).

>>>>> SUNO "STYLE / DESCRIPTION" KUTUSUNA <<<<<

warm playful educational underscore for a children's science cartoon, light orchestral
pop, soft marimba and pizzicato strings, gentle glockenspiel and celesta sparkle, soft
woodwinds (clarinet, flute), light hand percussion and soft claps, warm upright bass,
curious and bright, cozy Pixar-style wonder, hummable simple motif, major key, medium
tempo ~100 bpm, clean modern production, spacious mix that leaves room for a narrator,
dynamic but never busy. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics, no drops, no EDM.

>>>>> [STRUCTURE] (opsiyonel, Suno'ya sekans enerjisini vermek için) <<<<<
[Intro] soft curious marimba + glockenspiel, gentle rise, a little question in the melody
[Build] add pizzicato rhythm and light percussion, playful forward motion, growing warmth
[Peak] fuller warm orchestration for the discovery beat, bright but controlled
[Outro] settle back to soft marimba and celesta, warm resolved cadence, gentle fade

>>>>> NEGATIF (Exclude styles) <<<<<
no vocals, no singing, no rap, no lyrics, no aggressive drums, no distorted guitar,
no dubstep, no dark/tense cinematic, no horror, no lo-fi hiss.
