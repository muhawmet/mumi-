>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

Warm, playful instrumental underscore for a children's educational science cartoon, bright, curious and full of everyday discovery. Cozy Pixar-style wonder — soft marimba and pizzicato strings, gentle glockenspiel and celesta sparkle, light clarinet and flute, soft hand percussion and warm upright bass. Major key, medium tempo, hummable and spacious with plenty of room for a narrator, uplifting but never busy. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
