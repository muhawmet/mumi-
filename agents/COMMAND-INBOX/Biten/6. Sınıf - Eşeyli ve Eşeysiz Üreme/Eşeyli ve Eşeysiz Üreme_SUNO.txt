>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

Warm, gentle instrumental underscore for a children's educational science cartoon about how living things grow and multiply, tender and curious with a soft sense of life unfolding and quietly dividing. Cozy Pixar-style morning wonder — soft marimba and pizzicato strings, gentle glockenspiel and celesta sparkle, warm clarinet and flute, light hand percussion, soft nylon guitar and warm upright bass. Major key, medium-slow tempo, hummable and spacious with plenty of room for a narrator, tender and uplifting but never busy. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
