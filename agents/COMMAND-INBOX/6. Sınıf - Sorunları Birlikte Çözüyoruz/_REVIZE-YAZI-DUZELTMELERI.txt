K49 · defter sayfası
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the open notebook page, each list line is ONE single unbroken continuous graphite
wave running from the number straight to the right edge of the column — a single
stroke with no gaps, no separations, no clusters, no ascenders and no descenders
anywhere along it. There are no word shapes and no letter shapes of any kind on the
page. Keep the numerals 1 to 5 down the left margin and the one hard pencil
strike-through across item 4 exactly as they are, and keep the loose scribble block
in the lower right as it is. This is handwriting seen from too far away to read.


K45 · dilekçe gövde metni
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under the DİLEKÇE heading, each body line is ONE single unbroken continuous blue
ballpoint wave running the full width of the paragraph block — one stroke per line,
no gaps between groups, no clusters, no ascenders, no descenders, no letter shapes.
Keep the DİLEKÇE heading exactly as it is, sharp and unchanged. Keep the signature
line and the date line as two short single strokes. This is a filled form seen from
too far away to read.


K18 · kitap kapağındaki beyaz etiket
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The white rectangular label on the dark book cover is bare paper: a plain matte
sticker showing its own fibre grain, one softly lifted corner, a faint grey scuff
across the middle and a thin shadow along its lower edge. Its surface carries texture
only — no marks, no strokes, no lines, no symbols, nothing pen-made or printed
anywhere on it. Keep the book, the cover colour and the composition exactly as they are.


K24 · çuvallardaki baskı
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The stacked sacks in the lower-left pile show only woven jute: a coarse over-under
weave with visible slubs, a few loose fibre ends, dark stitching along the folded
seams, coal dust caught in the weave and one damp stain low on the nearest sack.
Their surfaces carry texture only — no marks, no strokes, no lines, no symbols,
nothing printed or stencilled anywhere on them. Keep the single printed KÖMÜR face
on the carried sack exactly as it is.


K32 · araç plakaları
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both number plates are dulled and unreadable by dirt: a film of road grime and dried
mud covers each plate edge to edge so the surface reads as one uniform grey-brown
tone with a few clean streaks where a wiper or a hand has passed. Their surfaces
carry texture only — no marks, no strokes, no lines, no symbols, nothing stamped or
printed anywhere on them, and no coloured band at either edge. Keep the cars, their
paint and their positions exactly as they are.
