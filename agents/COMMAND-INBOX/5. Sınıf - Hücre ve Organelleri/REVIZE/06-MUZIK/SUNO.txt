· >>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<
· --------------------------------------------------------------------

A clean modern minimalist underscore for a premium children's science film about a garden wall, an onion and the cells everything is built from. Felt piano leads: a short repeating motif of four notes, close-mic'd and intimate, with the felt dampers and the soft mechanical action of the keys audible in the room. The piece moves through three textures. It opens outdoors and acoustic — the felt piano almost alone, dry and close, with a single low sustained string note underneath and a very quiet sense of open air around it. In the middle it goes inward and electronic: the room disappears, a warm analog synth pad opens wide and slow, a soft filtered arpeggiated pulse begins to circle underneath, and the piano motif is answered by a single clean sine tone, weightless and suspended, as though the listener has shrunk into something very small. Toward the end the two worlds join — the pad stays but the dry close piano returns over it, a low sustained cello comes in warmly, and the motif from the opening resolves upward one last time. Built on space and patience rather than melody, contemporary Scandinavian neoclassical crossed with a modern product film: elegant, uncluttered, never sentimental. Tempo unhurried, around 74 BPM, gentle 4/4, no drum kit — only the soft filtered pulse and occasional low sub notes. The mid-range is deliberately kept clear so a narrator sits on top of it with nothing competing. The last fifteen seconds thin back to the felt piano alone over one held pad note and fade slowly to silence. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
