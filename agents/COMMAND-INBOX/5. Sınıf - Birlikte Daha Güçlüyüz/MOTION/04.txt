The camera arcs a few centimetres right around the hose mouth at waterline height, so the green crate slides toward the left edge and the funnel stays pinned at centre; it stops fully halfway through the clip and holds dead still after that. Exactly one person is in frame from first to last. The leaf stays one single leaf, the hi-vis vest keeps its bare reflective bands and gains no badge or brand name, and the funnel stays a smooth vortex rather than breaking into foam.
