The camera cranes down about a foot toward the wet cobbles, staying parallel to the rug so its shape never skews, and as it drops the mirrored rug and the two figures rise out of the reflection in the lower third; the move stops the moment that reflection is fully in frame. Half a second later the rug goes level and taut and the running water breaks apart into separate falling drops. By the end they stand straighter with their arms out and steady, both looking down at the middle of the stretched rug.
