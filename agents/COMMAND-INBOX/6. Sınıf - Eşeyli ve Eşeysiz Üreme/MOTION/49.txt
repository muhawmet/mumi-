Efe is one step past the threshold with his hand still on the door frame and his eyes on the basket. Then he lets go, takes a second unhurried step out onto the stone and stops with his weight settling onto both feet, and his look opens outward across the whole balcony — glass, basket, branch — his face easing into quiet recognition. On the branch the sparrow, which was perfectly still, hops once along the twig, flicks its tail and settles facing the balcony; the geranium leaves stir along the ledge and the lace curtain breathes in the doorway behind him. Camera pulls back and arcs gently leftward as he steps, the out-of-focus terracotta pot on the near ledge sweeping out through the bottom of frame as parallax, the move decelerating to a full stop at the exact instant he stops, the whole balcony now held inside the frame. The cat and the kittens stay asleep in the basket and no glow or floating light of any colour appears anywhere. Silent clip, no audio, no dialogue, mouth closed, no lip movement. No whip-pan, no shake, no snap-zoom, no camera warp.
