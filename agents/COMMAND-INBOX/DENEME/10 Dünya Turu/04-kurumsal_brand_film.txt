[1 LENS] 85mm lens at f/2.8, eye-level medium-close two-shot in three-quarter view; a painted steel handrail upright cuts the right edge of the frame in heavy defocus as the foreground anchor, so the composition sits off-axis and never becomes a centred symmetrical view.

[2 ÖZNE] The hero object is a printed assembly drawing lying flat on the wide top rail of a mezzanine walkway, held down against the draught by one heavy hex bolt. She has set the tip of a mechanical pencil on a single weld line and left it there without lifting it; his hand has already left the paper and is closing on the two-way radio clipped at his belt.

[3 KİMLİK] Both are Turkish, Anatolian features, and both faces read large in frame as they lean in over the drawing. She is about fifty-eight, grey hair pulled back tight, deep expression lines at the outer eye, skin holding pore-level micro-texture with no retouching and reading-glass marks either side of the nose; she wears a high-visibility vest over a plain navy shirt with one collar point bent up. He is about twenty-eight, close beard, a fine film of sweat at the temple, raw skin micro-texture across cheek and forehead; safety glasses are pushed up onto his head and a lanyard hangs inside his collar. Neither skin is smoothed and neither is tinted.

[4 MEKÂN] The setting is the mezzanine walkway of a working assembly hall on a Turkish industrial estate: full-height glazing at frame left, a painted steel handrail with chipped yellow edging, a coiled air line hooked over the rail, a stack of blue plastic bins on the floor below, and an open loading door at the far end letting a cold draught through.

[5 IŞIK] The full-height glazing at frame left is the only key, soft and directional and honest to the building's orientation; the linear LED strips over the hall are switched off. That daylight dies at the second handrail post, and past it the depth of the hall, the roof trusses and the far wall hold deep true black with no lifted grey. The dark painted steel wall at frame right acts as negative fill so the shadow side of both faces stays modelled rather than opened up; key-to-fill runs about four to one. Motivated light only, plus subtle 35mm film grain.

[6 SPEKÜLER OLAY] There is no concept light in this world; the bright events are all real speculars. The brushed aluminium rail returns one long anisotropic streak stretched along its grain, the bolt's machined chamfer returns a single hard point, and the paper returns nothing at all — which is exactly why the drawing reads as matte fibre sitting against metal.

[7 CANLI ÜÇLÜ] Three things are alive in the frame: the free corner of the drawing lifting and dropping in the draught from the loading door, fine dust turning slowly through the window light, and the pencil's pocket clip still rocking where she set it down.

[8 DERİNLİK] Depth in three layers — the defocused handrail upright across the right edge, the drawing and both faces sharp together on one plane as they lean in over it, and behind them the assembly hall dissolving into unresolved dark shapes with no readable machine and no other person.

[9 TEMAS] Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow: the bolt sits on the paper and dents it slightly under its own weight, the paper lies flat on the rail top with a narrow dark contact seam along its edge, and the bins below stand on the floor in their own shadows. Nothing floats.

STYLE: Premium corporate brand-film cinematography, floor-to-ceiling window daylight is the default soft key with direction honest to the building's orientation, fabric holds weave, falloff is real lens behavior, never painted blur, (5) GRADE, render institutional confidence as physics, coherent reflections and an unhurried camera. Clean digital-cinema edge definition with real micro-contrast, Skin holds pore-level micro-texture under any grain level, etched glass signage. Shallow depth of field (f/2.0–f/2.8) allowed for face and hero isolation with real optical falloff, Native 16:9 delivery. negative fill, motivated light, subtle 35mm film grain, raw skin micro-texture.
LIGHT AND PALETTE: shadows read as near-black cool blue, midtones read as bright cool blue, accents read as dusky warm burnt orange, highlights read as near-white cool blue — palette character: cool, architectural, restrained, dawn. steel-blue window key with one restrained amber accent held in real surfaces. NO teal-orange, NO neon, NO plastic gloss. Render these as light behaviour, never flat fills.
TEXT: The carrier is the drawing itself — a red rubber-stamp mark struck across its lower title block, the ink sitting down in the paper's tooth, dense and slightly smeared on the left where the pad was still wet and starved to a broken outline on the right, the whole mark pressed a few degrees off square by hand. It reads "REVİZE" (one word, six letters, the fourth letter a dotted capital İ). It sits in the near third of the drawing and its position is chosen so that neither her pencil nor his sleeve covers any letter. Any other print on the drawing or in the hall stays soft-focus, Turkish or blank.
