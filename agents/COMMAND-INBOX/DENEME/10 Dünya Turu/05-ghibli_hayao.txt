[1 LENS] 50mm lens at f/2.8, low child-eye-height close-medium three-quarter view from screen right; the rusted near post of the bus shelter is cropped hard through the left third of the frame in heavy defocus as a foreground anchor, so the composition never settles into a centred one-point view.
[2 ÖZNE] — @deniz sits on the shelter bench and looks down at the kraft paper bag on his knees: its wet bottom seam has opened and one plum is already past his shin, caught mid-roll on the gravel toward the road.
[3 KİMLİK] Warm matte tan skin, low specular, never tinted green or grey — @deniz is Turkish, around nine, cropped dark hair, a honey-cream cotton shirt with a terracotta-red collar band and cuffs rolled twice. Behind him @sevgi, his mother, Turkish, late thirties, a sage-green headscarf, stands half-turned to the enamel timetable plate; her face still reads clearly in three-quarter but her eyes are on the plate, not on the bag.
[4 MEKÂN] The setting is a rural Anatolian roadside bus shelter at mid-morning: a weathered timber bench with one replacement slat, an enamel timetable plate bolted to the back board, a galvanised milk can standing by the post, a wooden crate of plums with its lid tipped against the bench leg, and beyond the shelter a cut hay field running to a low stone wall.
[5 IŞIK] A single low raking morning sun from screen right skims the road and catches @deniz's cheek, the wet kraft paper and the top rim of the milk can; the shelter roof cuts it clean — the sun never reaches the back board, so the bench underside, the crate interior and @sevgi's lower half sit in deep warm burnt-orange shade. A soft cool-green bounce comes up off the hay field into the shaded side.
[7 CANLI ÜÇLÜ] Three things are alive in the frame: the directional wind combing the hay field and lifting the torn flap of the kraft bag all one way, the plum caught mid-roll on the gravel, and a slow cloud shadow crossing the far stone wall.
[8 DERİNLİK] Depth in three layers — the rusted shelter post cropped and blurred in the near left, @deniz and the failing bag sharp in the middle, and behind them the hay field under sculptural cumulus whose undersides are modelled in warm shade against a cobalt-to-cream sky.
[9 TEMAS] Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow; the plum touches the gravel with a small dark contact seam beneath it, the milk can's base sits flat in the dust, and the bag's torn corner hangs against @deniz's knee.
STYLE: Traditional 2D animation frame in the Hayao Miyazaki lineage of pastoral hand-painted theatrical features, Character key is hand-inked at 2-3px, Fills are flat cel in 2-3 value steps with matte finish, paper tooth catching raking light as micro-texture across the painted surface, (1) CLOUDS, always in silhouette at sky edge, STRICT PURE 2D CEL over painted watercolor-gouache backgrounds. Hand-inked line 2-3px, and they are thinner (1.5px equivalent) than the outline, painted edge and value separation only, Effects have loose. Paper grain overlay subtle across whole frame, 4:3 or 1.85:1 aspect.
LIGHT AND PALETTE: shadows read as deep warm burnt orange, midtones read as bright cool green, accents read as vivid warm red, highlights read as near-white warm ivory — palette character: sage-green + honey-cream + terracotta-red + sky-cobalt — Fuji-stock film warmth. Sky cobalt to cream-white in cumulus gradient. Shadow warm-brown, not neutral-grey. NO neon, NO teal-orange Hollywood grade, NO pure desaturated grey, NO acid-bright primary. Render these as light behaviour, never flat fills.
TEXT: The carrier is the kraft paper bag already on @deniz's knees — the greengrocer's own quick hand-brushed ink lettering across it reads "ERİK", four letters, one of them a dotted capital İ, the brush stroke thick where it turned and thinning to a dry skip at the last stroke, the ink soaking outward into the wet fibre at the torn bottom edge so the final letter softens and bleeds; the word sits across the upper face of the bag with @deniz's forearm clear of it; the enamel timetable plate behind stays soft-focus, Turkish or blank.
