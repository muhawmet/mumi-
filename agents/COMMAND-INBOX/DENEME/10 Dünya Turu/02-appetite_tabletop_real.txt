[2 ÖZNE] The hero object is the last cut slice of layered su böreği, lifted a hand's span above the tray and turning slightly, its leaves separating along the cut edge; her fingertips pinch one corner and the pastry visibly compresses where they hold it, while his open hand has stopped short over the empty grease ring where the slice sat.

[3 KİMLİK] Both are Turkish, Anatolian features, and both faces read large in frame as they lean over the tray. She is about thirty-eight, hair pushed back with the back of her wrist, raw skin micro-texture across cheek and nose with visible open pores, a steam-reddened patch on the pad of her thumb and flour caught in the creases of her knuckles; she wears a faded ink-blue apron over a plain long-sleeved shirt. He is about forty, short beard growing in grey at the chin, deep laugh lines at the outer eye, pore-level micro-texture with no retouching anywhere; he wears a grey wool jumper with a pilled cuff pushed up one forearm. Neither is smoothed and neither is tinted.

[4 MEKÂN] The setting is a narrow home kitchen in an old Istanbul flat in the morning: a chipped enamel tray, a sheet of kraft butcher paper gone translucent with butter, a squat tea glass with a used sugar spoon, a short stack of small plates, and a bunch of flat-leaf parsley still bound in its paper.

[5 IŞIK] One low raking window key enters from frame left at about twenty degrees elevation and skims across the crust so every blister and every torn leaf throws its own small shadow; that pool ends at the right edge of the tray, and past it the doorway and the far wall hold deep true black with no lifted grey. A black card just outside frame right acts as negative fill so the shadow sides of both faces stay modelled; key-to-fill runs about five to one. Motivated light only, plus subtle 35mm film grain.

[6 SPEKÜLER OLAY] There is no concept light here; the only bright event is a real specular one. The thin film of butter on the top leaf holds a single broken wet highlight that follows the pastry's blisters and disappears where the leaf is dry, and the steam is genuine backlit volume with its own thickness, not a painted plume.

[7 CANLI ÜÇLÜ] Three things are alive in the frame: the steam ribbon leaving the lifted slice, one flake of the top leaf detaching and dropping back toward the tray, and the grease ring on the paper still darkening outward at its edge where the slice was.

[8 DERİNLİK] Depth in three layers — the defocused tea-glass rim across the bottom right corner, the lifted slice and both faces sharp together on one plane, and behind them the kitchen dissolving into unresolved dark shapes with no readable object.

[9 TEMAS] Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow: the tray sits flat on the paper with the paper cockled up around its rim, the remaining crumbs lie in the tray with their own tiny shadows, the tea glass touches the table inside a faint wet ring, and nothing floats.
