[1 LENS] 85mm lens at f/4.0, eye-level medium-close two-shot in three-quarter view; the wire cage of a hanging drop light cuts across the near left edge of the frame in heavy defocus, so the composition sits off-axis and is never a centred one-point view.

[2 ÖZNE] The hero object is a dented cream enamel vacuum flask standing on the steel bench between them, and both of their hands are on it at the same moment: his fingers still wrapped low around the body he has held all night, her palm just closed over the shoulder above his knuckles, neither hand yet letting go.

[3 KİMLİK] Both are Turkish, Anatolian features, and both faces are large enough in frame to read. He is about fifty-five, close-cropped grey hair, a night's stubble, skin carrying pore-level micro-texture with visible open pores across the nose and a healed nick along one thumb, knuckles darkened with fine metal dust; he wears a faded navy work coat with a torn breast-pocket seam. She is about thirty, hair tied back under a knitted grey cap she has not taken off yet, cold-reddened ear tips, raw skin micro-texture on cheek and jaw with no retouching; she wears a charcoal fleece zipped to the collarbone. Neither skin is smoothed and neither is tinted; the light describes real texture, not polish.

[4 MEKÂN] The setting is a small Istanbul metalwork shop at twenty to six in the morning: a scarred steel bench with old layout scribe lines across it, a rack of vernier calipers hanging on bent nails, a coiled blue air hose, a cardboard tray of unlabelled brass fittings, and a curled wall calendar at the far end held with one drawing pin.

[5 IŞIK] A single low-wattage drop light hangs a hand's width above the bench and is the only motivated source in the shop; its pool ends at the left edge of the bench, and past that the shelves, the wall and the far end of the room fall to deep true black with no lifted grey. A black flag stands just outside frame right as negative fill, so the shadow side of both faces stays modelled rather than filled; key-to-fill runs about five to one. Motivated light only, plus subtle 35mm film grain.

[6 SPEKÜLER OLAY] There is no concept light in this world; the only bright events are real speculars. The enamel is not glossy — the lamp gives it one narrow soft-edged highlight along its upper shoulder, and the steel bench returns a stretched, broken reflection of the same bulb. The two highlights agree in position, shape and colour temperature because they come from one lamp.

[7 CANLI ÜÇLÜ] Three things are alive in the frame: a thin ribbon of steam leaving the flask's open cup-lid, the condensation ring on the bench still creeping outward at its edge where the flask stood a moment ago, and the drop light's cable swinging a few millimetres from being knocked.

[8 DERİNLİK] Depth in three layers — the defocused lamp cage across the near left edge, the flask and both faces sharp together on one plane, and behind them the shop dissolving into unresolved dark shapes with no readable object and no readable face.

[9 TEMAS] Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow: the flask's base sits inside its own wet ring, the flattened pad where enamel meets steel reads visibly darker than the shadow around it, and the calipers hang against the wall touching it.

STYLE: Turkish product and brand advertising, every highlight is a real specular event on the product's actual surface, (2) SURFACE TRUTH, matte plastic and paper hold microtexture, (4) CAMERA, f/4–f/8 for product sharpness and f/2.8 for a soft context, zero painted gloss, build desire from real surface physics. Clean digital-cinema edge definition with strong micro-contrast on material, glass caustic. Real optical falloff from the stated aperture (f/4–f/8 for product sharpness, f/2.8 for a soft lifestyle context), Delivered 16:9 by default. negative fill, motivated light, subtle 35mm film grain, raw skin micro-texture.
LIGHT AND PALETTE: shadows read as near-black neutral gray, midtones read as bright neutral gray, accents read as dusky warm red, highlights read as near-white cool indigo — palette character: clean, sculpted, reflective, neutral. controlled key shapes the form, one rim highlight separates the product. NO teal-orange, NO plastic gloss, NO oversaturation. Render these as light behaviour, never flat fills.
TEXT: The carrier is a strip of beige masking tape wrapped once around the flask's waist, furred at both torn ends — the letters are handwritten there in blue ballpoint marker by a tired hand, pressed hard so the ink pools where each stroke turns and skips where the tape is creased, running slightly uphill and not centred. It reads "GECE" (one word, four letters, all capitals, with no dot and no accent on any letter), facing camera on the flask's near side and clear of both hands. Any other print in the shop stays soft-focus, Turkish or blank.
NEGATIVE: the flask carries no logo, no emblem, no moulded lettering and no printed brand mark — its only marking is the taped strip; neither hand leaves the flask and no third hand enters the frame; the drop light stays one single lamp and never becomes two sources; NO wax-fake surfaces; NO painted gloss; NO teal-orange grade; NO plastic AI smoothing; NO English signage; NO floating overlay text, and imitation of any film-auteur signature; Turkish label only, printed as real diegetic matter on the packaging; NO fake plastic gloss or wax-fake surface — only the product's real finish and honest speculars; NO oversaturated ad gloss, NO neon; NO plastic AI-smooth skin on demonstrating hands — real pore and knuckle; NO fake AI bokeh or pasted background blur — only real optical falloff from the stated lens; NO floating UI or overlay text — any text is real printed diegetic matter on the product; NO film-auteur signature imitation — this is commercial advertising, not art cinema; no recognizable franchise or real-person characters, logos, brand names; the named thing replaced by a symbol for it — no icon, pictogram, chart, diagram, arrow, gauge or infographic panel standing in for the real object or the real action; the literal thing is IN the frame, photographed/rendered as matter, not illustrated as a concept; empty adjectives (cinematic, dynamic, stunning, 4K); flat slide; warped or drifting text; character retyping
