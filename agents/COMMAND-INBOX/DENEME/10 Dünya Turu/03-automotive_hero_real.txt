[1 LENS] 85mm lens at f/5.6, hip-height low three-quarter rear view of the car; the near rear tyre and its flattened contact patch cut the bottom left corner of the frame in soft focus as the foreground anchor, so the composition stays off-axis and never becomes a centred one-point view.

[2 ÖZNE] The hero object is the car itself, a mid-size estate in dark graphite paint still wet from the night, photographed as a designed object. At the rear door a driver drags his forearm across the fogged glass in one stroke, and inside, framed by the irregular oval he has just cleared, a passenger who has turned back from the wheel is looking straight out at him.

[3 KİMLİK] Both are Turkish, Anatolian features, and both faces read large enough to hold identity. He is about forty, three-day stubble catching the sky light along the jaw, pore-level micro-texture with visible open pores across the nose and a cold-reddened ear tip, cracked knuckles on the forearm he is using; he wears a dark quilted jacket with the cuff already soaked. She is about thirty-five, seen through the cleared glass, hair pushed behind one ear, raw skin micro-texture on the cheek with no retouching and a faint patch of breath fog on the inside of the glass beside her mouth; she wears a rust-red scarf loose at the collar. Neither skin is smoothed, neither is tinted.

[4 MEKÂN] The setting is a narrow residential street in Ankara before sunrise: wet asphalt with a repaired tar seam running under the car, a kerb of chipped concrete, a shuttered shopfront, a leafless plane tree, and a stacked plastic crate left beside a doorway. Everything behind the car stays unresolved in defocus with no readable object.

[5 IŞIK] The only sources are the cold pre-sunrise sky, acting as a broad soft key that wraps the upper body panels, and the car's own tail-lamp signature burning a dull warm red. The sky's wrap ends at the shoulder line: below it the doors, the wheel arch, the tyre and the street beyond fall to deep true black with no lifted grey. The dark shuttered wall just outside frame right works as negative fill so the shadow side of his face stays modelled; contrast runs about six to one. Motivated light only, plus subtle 35mm film grain.

[6 SPEKÜLER OLAY] There is no concept light in this world; every highlight is a reflected source and never paint. The sky lies along the shoulder line as one even dark-edged band of consistent width, the moving film of water on the door breaks that band into short vertical runs, and the tail lamp's red is repeated once, smaller and stretched, in the wet asphalt beneath the bumper — the two reds agree in position because they come from the same lamp.

[7 CANLI ÜÇLÜ] Three things are alive in the frame: a bead of water running down inside the cleared oval, his breath leaving him as visible vapour against the cold, and the stretched red reflection trembling in the water moving across the asphalt.

[8 DERİNLİK] Depth in three layers — the defocused rear tyre and its contact patch across the bottom left corner, the door panel with his face and her face sharp together on one plane forty centimetres apart across the glass, and the street behind dissolving into unresolved dark shapes.

[9 TEMAS] Every object rests in contact with its surface and casts a soft contact shadow: the tyre is visibly flattened where it meets the asphalt, the darkest continuous shape in the lower frame is that contact patch and the shadow under the sill, and the crate on the kerb sits in its own small pool of shadow. The car does not float.

STYLE: Turkish automotive advertising, Every highlight running along a shoulder line, shape the reflection, and the body reads as sculpted metal, A car lit with a direct key becomes a hot spot and a dead panel, so a highlight never simply bleaches the paint, tyre polish and a wet sheen when wet. Even dark line of consistent width, never a drawn stroke, The tyre's silhouette is the darkest continuous shape in the lower frame and anchors the whole image. negative fill, motivated light, subtle 35mm film grain, raw skin micro-texture.
LIGHT AND PALETTE: shadows read as near-black cool blue, midtones read as dusky neutral gray, accents read as dusky warm red, highlights read as near-white cool blue — palette character: sculpted, reflective, graphite, cold. the environment reflects along the body and one accent lives in the lamp signature. NO teal-orange grade, NO plastic gloss, NO oversaturation. Render these as light behaviour, never flat fills.
TEXT: The carrier is the car's own rear plate — a white pressed-aluminium Turkish plate with a narrow blue band down its left edge, the characters embossed first and then roller-painted black so paint sits only on the raised faces while the stamped edges show bare metal and road grime has collected in the valleys. It reads "34 ADK 725" — the digits 3 and 4, then three letters A, D and K, then the digits 7, 2 and 5, each group set apart by one clear space. It sits low in the frame beneath the tail lamp, unobstructed and square to its own panel. Any other Turkish print on the street stays soft-focus or blank.
NEGATIVE: the wheel is not invented — one consistent alloy pattern with real spoke thickness, a visible valve stem and brake dust on the barrel; the cleared oval on the glass has soft uneven hand-made edges and the fog outside it stays fog; the cabin behind the glass is a real lit interior with seats and headrests, never a black void; NO clay/toy proportion; NO floating car with no contact shadow; NO painted-on highlight; NO black-void glass; NO CGI-smooth asphalt; NO teal-orange grade; NO drone-epic canyon cliché, and any vehicle geometry invented from memory when a locked Brand Kit reference exists; NO outlines; NO English signage — Turkish label only, embossed or printed as real diegetic matter on plates, road signs and cabin displays; the contact shadow under each tyre and the flattened contact patch are mandatory; NO airbrushed specular streak, NO gloss added over the paint — every highlight is a reflected source; the cabin behind the glass is real and visible; NO featureless infinity ground; NO crushed noir contrast, NO neon wash over the body; NO drone-epic canyon or skyline flyover cliché; NO invented wheel, geometry is copied; NO toy or clay proportion, NO shrunken wheel, NO stretched wheelbase; no recognizable franchise or real-person characters, logos, brand names; the named thing replaced by a symbol for it — no icon, pictogram, chart, diagram, arrow, gauge or infographic panel standing in for the real object or the real action; the literal thing is IN the frame, photographed/rendered as matter, not illustrated as a concept; empty adjectives (cinematic, dynamic, stunning, 4K); flat slide; warped or drifting text; character retyping
