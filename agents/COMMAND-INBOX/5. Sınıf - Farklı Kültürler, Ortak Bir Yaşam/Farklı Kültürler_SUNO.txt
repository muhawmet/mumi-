>>> SUNO "SIMPLE" KUTUSUNA — SADECE ŞUNU YAPIŞTIR <<<

A warm, gentle instrumental score for a children's educational film about two girls from
different countries discovering that their cultures share the same wooden flute. Begin sparse
and a little lonely — a single breathy end-blown wooden flute alone over soft sustained
strings, like a schoolyard in late afternoon. A second flute answers it and the two lines
gradually braid into one melody. Build slowly and warmly with a small acoustic ensemble:
wooden flute, gentle clarinet, soft nylon-string guitar, hand frame drum with a light
unhurried pulse, warm cello, high sustained strings, a few soft mallet notes. Anatolian and
Balkan folk colour in the melody but never a heavy dance rhythm — this stays tender and
spacious. Mid-piece it opens into a hopeful, generous swell as the ensemble joins, then pulls
all the way back at the end to the same single flute, quieter and settled. Slow to moderate
tempo, major key, hopeful and human, nothing triumphant or cinematic-epic. Plenty of room for
a narrator throughout — the melody stays out of the mid-range where speech sits. Instrumental
only, no vocals, no lyrics.
